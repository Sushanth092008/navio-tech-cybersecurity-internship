## Debrief

This exercise demonstrates common social-engineering signals, including urgency, unexpected account notices, and requests to act through a message. The goal is education, not to shame or grade participants.

Key defensive actions:
- Check the complete sender address.
- Be cautious of urgency or threats.
- Verify requests through a known independent channel.
- Do not enter secrets after following an unsolicited message.
- Report suspicious messages through the organization's approved process.

Study findings must be aggregated and anonymized. Retain data only for the approved period and delete it according to the approved protocol.
