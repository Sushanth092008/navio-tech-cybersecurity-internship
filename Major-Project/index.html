LEARNING CHECKLISTDEFENSIVE GUIDANCE
1. Sender identity

Check the full sender address and compare it with a known, independently verified contact channel.

2. Urgency and pressure

Unexpected deadlines, threats, and pressure to act immediately are common social-engineering signals.

3. Links and destinations

Do not follow unexpected links. Navigate to a service using a trusted bookmark or manually entered known address.

4. Requests for secrets

Never enter passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes, or payment details into a page reached through an unsolicited message.

5. Reporting

Use the organization's approved report-phishing process or contact its IT/security team through a verified channel.

Research and ethics protocol

Before any participant study, obtain instructor and institutional approval, explain the exercise and data collected, obtain informed consent, allow withdrawal, minimize data, and debrief participants.
